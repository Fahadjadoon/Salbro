Who We Are

About Salbro
General Contracting

Salbro General Contracting L.L.C. is a premier multidisciplinary contracting firm and workforce technical partner serving major energy, construction, and power mega-projects across the UAE. Established on February 8, 2022, in Abu Dhabi, we have rapidly emerged as a trusted provider of comprehensive civil construction services, oilfield technical solutions, and skilled manpower supply.

Under the dynamic leadership of our Founder & CEO, Sajid Mehmood, we combine operational flexibility with a strong quality management system to deliver outstanding service, reliability, and complete customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To provide outstanding service through a strong quality management system and a team of competent professionals.

Our Mission

To deliver quality services on time, with professionalism, reliability, and complete customer satisfaction.
